01
— paradigm shift

From Producer to Curator: The Designer's Role in AI/UX

AI can generate UI, flows, and prototypes — but generation is not design. The designer's core value shifts from crafting polished screens to judging what to keep, what to change, and why. This course does not teach prompt tricks; it turns your design judgment into durable, machine-readable knowledge assets.

Core Thesis
Your existing design assets are the best starting point for a knowledge base. There is no standard answer here, only the right answer for your context. You leave after four weeks with a running operational system, not another slide deck.

First Prompt→UI: Establishing a Baseline Impression

Pedagogical Note: We deliberately hold back UICrit here. 01
— research-based audit

UICrit: From Google Research to Your Audit Standards

UICrit is an open-source dataset published by Google Research at UIST '24 — featuring 3,059 expert critiques across 983 mobile UIs. We filter this into four core dimensions as our audit foundation. We avoid prescriptive guidelines like Material or HIG because we need an evaluative framework (auditing user intent), not a prescriptive one (auditing styling conventions).

dimension 1
Information Architecture
UICrit · Information Architecture
Page hierarchy, grouping logic, and navigation structure that allow users to predict the next step.
hierarchy grouping navigation
dimension 2
Accessibility
UICrit + WCAG 2.2
4.5:1 contrast ratios, focus order, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility. Pairing UICrit commentary with WCAG technical benchmarks.
contrast focus screen reader
dimension 3
Progressive Disclosure
UICrit · Progressive Disclosure
Does information density scale with the user's task stage? Dumping all options at once makes AI outputs look superficially complete but practically unusable.
layering defaults complexity
dimension 4
Interface Copy
UICrit · UX Copy
Button labels, error states, and empty state messaging — where AI most frequently outputs "correct but useless" text. Copy requires product context to work, which is why document→UI narrows variance most on this dimension.
labels errors empty states tone
Why Not Material Design
Material Design is prescriptive — it mandates rounded corners and 8px grid spacing. UICrit is evaluative — it asks whether this grouping helps the user complete their task. Auditors need evaluative frameworks to write substantive contradiction reports rather than complaining that something isn't Material style.

Managing Variance
Learners produce three distinct curves: prompt (widest), screenshot (middle), and document (narrowest, yet still variable). They don't need lectures warning that outputs drift; they have seen it live. What they need is knowing what to do when drift happens — the two convergence steps below provide the answer.
03
— convergence · design system

From Variants to Design System: Converging Tokens × Components × Patterns

Extract top-performing elements across the three variant sets and assemble a starter design system in Pencil. Focus on three clean layers: Design Tokens (color, typography, spacing), Components (buttons, inputs, cards), and Patterns (navigation, forms, empty states). Log every convergence decision in design decision records.

04
— wiki gap repair

Wiki Gap Repair: Design Decision Records

Side-by-side auditing exposes wiki blind spots: some criteria are too vague ("clear IA" is useless; specify "primary navigation capped at 5 items, secondary items collapsed by default"); some product context was missing (tone stated "professional" but omitted "explain one extra step for novice users"). Patch exposed gaps back into wiki/decisions/.

Design Decision Records (decisions/) are the highest-value wiki assets: they answer why a pattern was chosen over alternatives. In future iterations, agents read not just "use this", but "use this because of X constraint" — enabling sound extrapolation in novel scenarios.

04
— core thesis

Core Course Theses

The Variance Gradient
Three paths (prompt→UI, screenshot→UI, document→UI) establish a variance gradient. A knowledge base does not eliminate variance; it narrows variance into a manageable review range. Learners validating this live in Weeks 2–3 is the load-bearing pillar of the course.
Evaluative > Prescriptive
UICrit + WCAG are evaluative frameworks — they ask "does this design help users complete tasks?", not "does this follow a specific company's UI guide?". Designer audit criteria should rest on empirical research rather than vendor style dogma.
Wiki as Interface
The wiki is not static human documentation — it is the interface between human designer and AI agent. Product context, audit criteria, design decisions, and tokens cross-reference to give agents complete situational awareness. Wiki is the interface; Agent is the worker.
